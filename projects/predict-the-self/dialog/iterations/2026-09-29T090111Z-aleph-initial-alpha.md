## Limitations and decisions

- This family was fixed after its constituent analyses and aggregate results
  were known. The stress test cannot retroactively preregister earlier choices
  or convert dependent evidence into untouched confirmation.
- Synchronized resampling preserves empirical dependence among stored case
  effects but does not refit the heavily overlapping leave-one-out folds. The
  intervals describe sensitivity to this selected cohort's case composition,
  not prospective familywise control or population generalization.
- An interval containing zero is inconclusive rather than evidence of
  equivalence. The exact line-count point tie is descriptive, not a stable
  directional effect.
- No benchmark row, development answer, or private answer was read by the new
  calculation; no prediction or test artifact changed. The contrast audit is
  derived from benchmark-data adaptations under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
