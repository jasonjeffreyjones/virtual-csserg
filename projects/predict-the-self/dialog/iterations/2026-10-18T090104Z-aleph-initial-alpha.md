## Limitations and decisions

- This is publication assurance, not a new estimate of predictability. No
  prediction, metric, scientific interpretation, benchmark artifact, or
  private-test claim changed.
- The two ablations remain dependent, training-only analyses using overlapping
  folds and representations chosen after earlier Project results were known.
  Source verification does not make them untouched confirmation, establish a
  lexical mechanism, or supply population evidence.
- The contract now covers 157 Executive Summary values. The simultaneous-
  interval stress-test and calibrated-synthesis tables, secondary Full Report
  tables, and quantitative short-report statements remain outside it.
- No browser executable is installed on this host, so rendered desktop,
  mobile, keyboard, and assistive-technology inspection remains outstanding.
