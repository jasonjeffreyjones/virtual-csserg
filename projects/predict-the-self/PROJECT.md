# Predict the Self

A formidable question is this: How predictable are human lives?

In search of an answer, in this project we will address a series of more tractable questions. Specifically, we will answer the following research questions:

- Given the same individuals describing themselves one year apart, how much better than simple baselines can we do in the task of predicting Year 2 description given the Year 1 description?
- Given a large set of identity signifiers and longitudinal observations, how do they rank from most enduring to most ephemeral?
- Given longitudinal observations, which signifier-to-signifier transmutations are most frequent?
