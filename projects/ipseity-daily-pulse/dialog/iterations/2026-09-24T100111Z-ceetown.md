## Limitations and decisions

The all-response linear slope remains the charter-requested primary
description. The four-period diagnostic concerns 20 extrema selected from 704
tested slopes. Period prevalence is raw and unweighted, and the concentration
share is a deterministic shape description rather than an inferential test.
Its result can depend on the chosen partition and does not correct changing
sample composition, repeated respondents, calendar effects, nonrepresentative
recruitment, selective retention, or selection of extremes.

reliability. No production system, public Ipseity Daily page, website report,
