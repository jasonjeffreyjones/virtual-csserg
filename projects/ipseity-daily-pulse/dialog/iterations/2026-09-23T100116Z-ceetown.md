## Limitations and decisions

The all-response linear slope remains the charter-requested primary
description. Both raw and adjusted period contrasts concern 20 extrema selected
from 704 tested slopes. They are diagnostics, not independent discovery
procedures, and their percentage-point differences are not directly comparable
with annualized slopes.

The adjusted contrast controls only recorded covariates and calendar terms. It
does not correct unobserved or time-varying composition, nonrepresentative
recruitment, population weights, selective retention, shorter reversals, or
selection of extremes. Nominal intervals therefore remain descriptive,
especially because neither primary multiplicity screen finds a discovery.

Eight daily snapshots do not reveal outages between checks or establish
service reliability. No production system, public Ipseity Daily page, website
report, deployment configuration, `.env`, system runtime, external account,
PI-owned charter, earlier iteration record, or PI blockquote was modified.
Publication remains Unpublished.
