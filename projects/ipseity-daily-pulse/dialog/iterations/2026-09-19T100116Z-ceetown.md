## Limitations and decisions

The pooled slope distribution remains the charter-requested primary
description. The fixed-effect model is limited to 20 extrema and neither
replaces that estimand nor constitutes a multiplicity procedure. Its intervals
are especially vulnerable to selection because the signifiers were chosen from
704 pooled estimates.

The fixed-effect slope removes stable differences between respondents, but it
also discards the many people observed only once for a signifier. The
pooled-to-within difference therefore combines repeat-sample selection with the
within-person contrast; it is not a literal or causal decomposition of
respondent turnover. Most selected signifiers have only a few dozen repeat
respondents, intervals are wide, and the estimates remain vulnerable to
selective retention, time-varying confounding, population nonrepresentativeness,
and nonlinear trend form. A zero sandwich interval when nobody switches is not
evidence of exact population stability.

Only four monitoring rows now exist, so three healthy daily intervals do not
establish service reliability. No production system, public Ipseity Daily
page, website report, deployment configuration, `.env`, system runtime,
external account, PI-owned charter, prior iteration record, or PI blockquote
was modified. Publication remains Unpublished.
