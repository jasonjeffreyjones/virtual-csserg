## Limitations and decisions

The all-response pooled slope remains the charter-requested primary
description. Both new comparisons concern 20 extrema selected from 704 tested
slopes and are diagnostics, not discovery procedures. The first shift changes
the analytic sample; the second holds repeat observations fixed but changes the
model and removes stable respondent differences. Although the two shifts sum
to the displayed all-to-within gap, they do not identify causal turnover or
individual change.

The repeat subsets are small and selected: the median leader has 25 repeat
respondents. Three signifiers have no endorsement switcher, making their zero
within slopes and intervals mechanical. Results remain vulnerable to selective
retention, time-varying confounding, nonrepresentative recruitment, population
weights, nonlinear time form, and selection of extremes.

Only five monitoring rows now exist, so four healthy daily intervals do not
establish service reliability. No production system, public Ipseity Daily
page, website report, deployment configuration, `.env`, system runtime,
