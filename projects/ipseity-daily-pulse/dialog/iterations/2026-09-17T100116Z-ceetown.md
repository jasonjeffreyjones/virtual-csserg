## Limitations and decisions

Clustering changes estimated uncertainty, not the unweighted slope itself. It
addresses dependence among answers by the same hashed respondent but not
changing sample composition, calendar structure, population weights, or model
misspecification. The original Benjamini–Hochberg guarantee is not unrestricted
under dependent tests; signifier answers share respondents, so the more
conservative Bonferroni result is reported alongside it. Large point estimates
and nominal clustered intervals that exclude zero are not promoted as
discoveries when both multiplicity screens yield zero.
