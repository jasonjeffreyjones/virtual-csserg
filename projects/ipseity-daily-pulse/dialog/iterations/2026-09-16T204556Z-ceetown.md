## Limitations and decisions

The first history row is a baseline; it cannot yet identify a continuing outage
or support a meaningful across-check trend plot. The cumulative growth figure
therefore uses observation dates inside the validated microdata. Future daily
checks should add history-based longitudinal evidence once multiple checks
exist.

Signifier slopes are unweighted linear probability estimates, not causal or
population-weighted effects. Their ordinary intervals do not adjust for
repeated respondents, changing sample composition, calendar structure, or the
selection of extremes from 704 estimates. The leader tables are monitoring
leads rather than claims that US adult self-concepts changed at the displayed
rates. This caution is explicit in the current findings.

No production system, public Ipseity Daily page, website report, deployment
configuration, `.env`, system runtime, external account, or PI-owned charter
was modified. Publication remains Unpublished; the outreach copy remains an
internal draft.
