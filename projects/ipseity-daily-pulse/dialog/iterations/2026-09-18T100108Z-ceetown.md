## Limitations and decisions

The unadjusted slope distribution remains the primary charter-requested
description. The new model is deliberately limited to the 20 extrema and
therefore does not replace the primary estimand or constitute an additional
multiplicity procedure. Nominal adjusted intervals are especially vulnerable
to selection because the signifiers were chosen from 704 estimates.

Adjustment covers observed age and selected categorical composition plus
simple weekday and annual seasonality. It does not address unobserved
composition, respondent turnover, nonrepresentative recruitment, population
weights, time-varying confounding, or nonlinear trend form. Directional
stability for all 20 leaders is therefore weaker evidence than it may sound;
the sizeable coefficient shifts and wide intervals reinforce the decision not
to present the rankings as population change.

Only three monitoring rows now exist, so two healthy daily intervals do not
establish service reliability. No production system, public Ipseity Daily
page, website report, deployment configuration, `.env`, system runtime,
external account, PI-owned charter, prior iteration record, or PI blockquote
was modified. Publication remains Unpublished.
