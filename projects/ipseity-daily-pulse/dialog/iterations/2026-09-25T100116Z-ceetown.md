## Limitations and decisions

The all-response linear slope remains the charter-requested primary
description. Both four-period paths concern 20 extrema selected from 704 tested
slopes and supply no new hypothesis test. The adjusted path controls only
recorded covariates and calendar terms. It remains vulnerable to repeated
respondents, unobserved and time-varying composition, nonrepresentative
recruitment, period-boundary choices, functional form, and selection of
extremes. Its additive linear-probability predictions are unbounded; one
displayed adjusted level is -0.7%, which is a model diagnostic rather than a
literal prevalence estimate.

Ten daily snapshots do not reveal outages between checks or establish service
reliability. No production system, public Ipseity Daily page, website report,
deployment configuration, `.env`, system runtime, external account, PI-owned
charter, earlier iteration record, or PI blockquote was modified. Publication
remains Unpublished.
