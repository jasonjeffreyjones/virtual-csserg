# Nearly 700,000 answers about identity

**Draft status:** Internal; not published or approved by Dr. Jones.

## Suggested post

How do American adults describe themselves? Ipseity Daily asks a fresh survey
sample whether signifiers such as `dad`, `trusting`, or `Cleveland Browns fan`
describe them today. The canonical data through September 15, 2026 contain
699,835 Yes/No answers across 707 signifiers and 4,979 hashed respondents.

That is response volume—not 699,835 people, and not by itself a population
trend. The openly downloadable data make both the scale and the caveats
inspectable.

Source: [Ipseity Daily download page](https://jasonjones.ninja/social-science-dashboard-inator/ipseity-daily/download.html)
