Research focus / Predict the Self

Regression to the mean is not yet a reliable edge.

A cross-fitted length forecast improves descriptively, but its paired interval spans zero.

In 150 leave-one-out training cases, a regression-to-the-mean word-count distribution reaches CRPS 31.572068 versus 32.579884 for a raw no-source distribution. Its +1.007816 reduction has interval −1.235220 to +3.013792. The model corrects the much worse unit-slope persistence assumption, but it does not establish incremental response-length skill from the focal person's earlier answer. Private test results for the frozen text submission are still pending.
