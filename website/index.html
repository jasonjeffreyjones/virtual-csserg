Research focus / Predict the Self

Earlier words predict response length better than future words.

A locked 150-case leave-one-out ablation finds most supported signal about later self-description length in the person's earlier text.

Text-only neighborhoods improve follow-up word-count CRPS over a source-calibrated marginal distribution. Demographics alone do not, although they add a smaller incremental gain when combined with text. The same neighborhood still loses to common additions when choosing future tokens. This is dependent cross-validation on method-development data, not fresh confirmation, and private test results remain pending.
